# SRGC Developments

Build a premium, modern, conversion-focused website for SRGC Developments Ltd, a commercial and residential contracting company serving Ontario, Canada.



Brand identity



Use the uploaded SRGC Developments Ltd logo as the primary brand identity. Preserve the original logo, proportions, colours, and typography. Do not recreate or distort it.



Visual direction:



- Premium architectural and construction-industry aesthetic.

- Navy blue, steel blue, white, and light grey colour palette inspired by the logo.

- Strong typography, generous spacing, clean layouts, and subtle animations.

- Professional, confident, established, and trustworthy.

- Avoid generic construction templates, excessive gradients, cartoon icons, and cluttered layouts.



Website structure



1. Hero section

Create a striking full-width hero featuring a high-quality photograph of a modern commercial or residential building exterior.



Headline:

"Building Better. Delivering Excellence."



Supporting text:

"Commercial and Residential Contracting Solutions Across Ontario."



Primary CTA: "Request a Quote"

Secondary CTA: "Explore Our Services"



Include a subtle dark overlay on the hero image to ensure text readability. Add a refined entrance animation.



2. About SRGC

Headline: "Your Partner in Quality Construction"



Introduce SRGC Developments Ltd as a contractor providing commercial and residential contracting services across Ontario.



Keep the copy professional and concise. Focus on workmanship, attention to detail, reliability, and quality execution without making unsupported claims about years of experience, certifications, or project history.



3. Services

Create four visually distinct service cards with high-quality relevant imagery:



- Stucco – Professional stucco exterior finishing solutions.

- EIFS – Exterior insulation and finish systems.

- Siding – Exterior siding installation and finishing.

- Drywall – Drywall installation and interior finishing.



Each card should have a subtle hover effect and a short description.



4. Commercial & Residential

Create a visually engaging split section showcasing both commercial and residential contracting.



Commercial: Highlight exterior and interior contracting solutions for commercial properties.



Residential: Highlight exterior finishing and interior drywall solutions for residential properties.



Use architectural photography rather than generic stock illustrations.



5. Why Choose SRGC

Create a clean, elegant section highlighting:



- Commercial and residential contracting

- Multiple specialized services

- Quality-focused workmanship

- Serving clients across Ontario



Do not invent testimonials, awards, certifications, or statistics.



6. Project showcase

Create an attractive image gallery featuring commercial buildings, residential exteriors, stucco finishes, siding, and drywall work.



Use realistic, high-quality construction photography as temporary visual content. Do not present stock photographs as confirmed SRGC projects. Make the gallery easy to replace with actual project photographs later.



7. Contact section

Headline: "Let's Build Something Together."



Display the following contact information prominently:



Company: SRGC Developments Ltd

Contact: Berat Sorguc

Phone: 647-721-4804

Email: info@srgcdevelopments.ca

Service area: Ontario, Canada



Add:



- A clickable phone number.

- A clickable email address.

- A simple enquiry form with Name, Email, Phone, Service Required, and Message.

- A prominent "Request a Quote" button.



8. Footer

Include the SRGC logo, company name, service links, contact details, and copyright notice.



Design and functionality requirements



- Fully responsive on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

- Sticky navigation with smooth scrolling.

- Functional mobile navigation menu.

- All CTA buttons should navigate to the contact section or relevant action.

- Use subtle scroll-triggered animations and elegant hover transitions.

- Optimise images and maintain fast loading.

- Use semantic HTML and SEO-friendly headings.

- Add appropriate page title and meta description.

- Ensure good accessibility, contrast, and readable typography.



Important implementation instructions



Build this as a polished single-page website.

Keep the code clean and reusable.

Do not add unnecessary pages, complex backend functionality, fake reviews, invented project details, or unsupported company claims.

Prioritise visual quality, professional presentation, and generating genuine quote enquiries.
